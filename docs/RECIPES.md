# Recipe progression (hard mode)

These costs are intentionally high and represent a **new original Pylon/Rebar crafting economy**. The included Talismans 2026.45 JAR has Tier II recipes requiring 8 Tier I copies and Tier III recipes requiring 8 Tier II copies, with rare central cores. We use the same pattern and substitute our own Rebar items and Pylon materials.

| Item | 3x3 shape | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Relic Matrix | ABA / BKB / ABA | 4 Pylon bronze ingots, 4 gold blocks, 1 Eye of Ender |
| Talisman Core I | ABA / BKB / ABA | 4 diamond blocks, 4 Pylon steel ingots, 1 Eye of Ender |
| Talisman Core II | ABA / BKB / ABA | 4 Pylon diamond dust, 4 netherite scrap, 1 Heart of the Sea |
| Talisman Core III | ABA / BKB / ABA | 4 Pylon intermediate core chunks, 4 netherite ingots, 1 Nether Star |
| Any tier II | PPP / PKP / PPP | 8 copies of same family's Tier I and Core II |
| Any tier III | PPP / PKP / PPP | 8 copies of same family's Tier II and Core III |

## Tier I family recipes

- Archery: 4 bows, 4 crossbows, Core I.
- Strength: iron block, diamond sword, gold block, bow, crossbow, netherite ingot, trident, emerald block, Core I.
- Attack Speed: 4 sugar, 4 diamond swords, Core I.
- Boss: 4 bells, 4 Wither Skeleton skulls, Core I.
- Poseidon: 4 nautilus shells, 4 tridents, Core I.
- Resistance: 4 iron blocks, 4 obsidian, Core I.
- Immunity Ring: 4 poisonous potatoes, 4 golden apples, Core II.
- Necrosis Ring: 4 Wither Roses, 4 ancient debris, Core II.
- Angel: 4 ghast tears, 4 phantom membranes, Core I.
- Traveller: 4 diamond boots, 4 rabbit feet, Core I.
- Firefighter: 4 blaze rods, 4 magma creams, Relic Matrix.
- Lava Walker: 4 obsidian, 4 netherite scraps, Relic Matrix.
- Anvil: 4 anvils, 4 diamond blocks, Relic Matrix.

No recipes create new Experience, Water Breathing, Farming, Hunting, Luck, Health, Breeding, Enchanting, Bartering or Sharpness talismans because they overlap Pylon or the existing Strength relic.

**Balance note:** 64 base talismans and eight Tier II cores are needed to forge a single Tier III relic, as well as its own Core III. On an established multiplayer server, this is an endgame sink. Consider adjusting costs with a versioned recipe migration rather than silently changing recipes that players already learned.

## Whirlwind (safe arrow deflection)

- Tier I: 4 Breeze Rods + 4 Pylon Steel Sheets + Talisman Core I.
- Tier II: 8 Whirlwind I + Talisman Core II.
- Tier III: 8 Whirlwind II + Talisman Core III.

Tier III requires 64 Tier I Whirlwind talismans (256 Breeze Rods, 256 steel sheets, and the cores used for those 64 Tier I items), eight Core II, and one Core III.
This protects against arrows without generating additional projectiles or altering trident pickup.
